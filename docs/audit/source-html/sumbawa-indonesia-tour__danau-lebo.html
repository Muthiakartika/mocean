Why Visit Danau Lebo

Danau Lebo is a wide, calm lake near Taliwang, the main town of West Sumbawa. Hills and palms frame the water, and local fishermen still work it from small wooden boats. Most visitors come for a canoe ride or a slow walk along the shore, and many pair it with the viewpoint at the Village Above the Clouds.

Take Your Time

A Pause in Every View

The lake’s calm waters and soft breezes invite you to slow down and simply enjoy the moment. This peaceful lake in Sumbawa is surrounded by natural beauty, offering a gentle escape from busy days.

Walking along the shore, you might spot local fishermen at work or hear the calls of birds that make this lake their home. There are quiet corners perfect for picnics, shaded spots for resting, and open views that seem to stretch forever. Danau Lebo Taliwang is a place to breathe deeply, connect with nature, and carry home a sense of peace.

A Hidden Place Waiting to Be Found

A secret retreat where time slows down

Unlike destinations crowded with tourists, this lake remains a secluded retreat. Nestled between rolling hills and lush greenery, it offers an authentic view of Sumbawa’s beauty. The gentle wind brushing across the lake and the sight of traditional fishing boats passing by add to its charm. It’s a place where visitors can slow down, enjoy nature at its purest, and escape the pressures of everyday life.

FAQ

Danau Lebo FAQ

Where is Danau Lebo?

Danau Lebo is a freshwater lake near Taliwang, the main town of West Sumbawa Regency in West Nusa Tenggara, Indonesia.

Can you canoe on Danau Lebo?

Yes. Our Danau Lebo Lake Canoeing tour takes about four hours and glides across the lake past local fishermen and their wooden boats.

Can I combine Danau Lebo with another trip?

Yes. The Village Above the Clouds & Danau Lebo tour pairs a mountain viewpoint with a canoe ride on the lake, with no minimum group size.

How do I book a Danau Lebo tour?
