Sumbawa Surf Camp

A Surf Camp in West Sumbawa for Every Level

Join us for a Mocean surf trip in West Sumbawa, where the waves are consistent and the line-ups are small. It’s a good fit for surfers of all levels who want to sharpen their skills on a mix of waves. Each spot is picked to help you progress, and our Sumbawa surf camp mixes well-known breaks with a few quieter ones.

The Breaks on Our Doorstep

Northern Rights

Northern Rights breaks right in front of the resort. It’s a right-hander with steeper faces and tighter sections, best on mid to high tide when the lines stack up clean. It rewards commitment and sharp turns, especially when the wind swings offshore.

Middle Reef

Middle Reef sits between Northern Rights and Dirty Hippies. Softer take-offs and mellow shoulders make it our spot for warm-ups, beginner lessons and first green waves.

Dirty Hippies

Dirty Hippies is a laid-back left about 15 minutes’ walk down the beach. Longer walls and a slower pace, good for flowing lines and cutbacks.

Day Trips to Scar Reef, Yo-Yos and Supersuck

When the swell and wind line up, we can arrange transport to other West Sumbawa breaks such as Scar Reef, Yo-Yos and Supersuck. See our Sumbawa surf spots guide for levels and seasons.

Where Waves Meet Adventure

A Day at Surf Camp

Most days start with a dawn check of Northern Rights from the beach. Surf the morning glass, come in for breakfast at Mutiara, then rest through the heat of the day by the pool or on a yoga mat. We look at the tide and wind with you and pick the afternoon session, often Middle Reef for progression or Dirty Hippies when the left is working. Evenings are sunset, dinner and talking through the day’s waves.

FAQ

Surf Camp FAQ

Is the Sumbawa surf camp good for beginners?

Yes. Beginners learn at Middle Reef, which has softer take-offs and mellow shoulders. Private lessons with a local coach cost IDR 1,000,000 per person, for up to two people.

When is the best season to surf West Sumbawa?

April to October is the most consistent, with dry-season swell and offshore winds in the mornings. Waves still break the rest of the year, with fewer people in the water.

Which surf breaks can I walk to from Mocean?

Northern Rights is right in front of the resort, Middle Reef is next to it, and Dirty Hippies is about a 15-minute walk down the beach.

Can I rent a surfboard?

Yes, boards are IDR 150,000 a day. Tell us your height, weight and level before you arrive and we’ll set one aside.

How is this different from a Bali surf camp?

Smaller crowds. West Sumbawa has a handful of resorts along the coast, so you share Northern Rights with a few people instead of a full line-up.
