Peak Performance Buoyancy

At Mocean Beach Resort, our Peak Performance Buoyancy course helps certified divers refine control, positioning, and movement underwater. Conducted in the clear, calm waters of West Sumbawa, Indonesia, this course focuses on precision and efficiency to make every dive effortless. With just two divers per instructor, you receive personalized guidance for improving trim, buoyancy, and air consumption. Practice in a diver-centered environment with private lockers, a wet area, and an air-conditioned camera room. Experience the freedom of gliding smoothly over vibrant marine life, including manta rays, reef sharks, and eagle rays.

Tailored Skill Sessions

Your PPB Course

This one-day course includes two boat dives focused on mastering buoyancy control. Each dive emphasizes controlled movements, precise hovering, and efficient underwater navigation. Divers learn to maintain perfect trim and handle delicate marine environments with care, enhancing both confidence and control beneath the surface. By the end of the course, you’ll be confident in your underwater control and ready to tackle advanced dives with ease.

Facilities Built for Divers

Dive Center Excellence

Our dive center is fully equipped to support peak performance buoyancy training. Spacious wet areas, private lockers, and an air-conditioned camera room ensure your comfort and gear safety. Every detail is designed to create a seamless, focused training environment where divers can practice, review, and perfect their skills.

FAQ

Peak Performance Buoyancy FAQ

Who can take the PPB course?

Any certified diver can join, whether you’re Open Water certified or have advanced experience.

What skills will I learn?

You’ll refine buoyancy, trim, finning techniques, and air consumption to glide smoothly and efficiently underwater.

How large are the training groups?

Groups are limited to two divers per instructor for personalized guidance and hands-on practice.

Where does the training take place?

The course combines controlled exercises and open water dives in calm, clear waters around West Sumbawa.

What facilities support the course?

Our dive center provides private lockers, wet areas, and an air-conditioned camera room for equipment and comfort.
