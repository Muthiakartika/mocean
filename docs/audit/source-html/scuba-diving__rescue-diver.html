Rescue Diver

Dive Into Advanced Rescue Skills

PADI Rescue Diver

At Mocean Beach Resort, our PADI Rescue Diver course prepares certified divers to manage dive emergencies with confidence. This course takes place in the pristine waters of West Sumbawa, Indonesia, where experienced instructors guide you through realistic rescue scenarios and problem-solving exercises. Dive with just two certified divers per instructor for a personalized learning experience. Expect encounters with diverse marine life, including manta rays, reef sharks, and eagle rays, in visibility up to 15 meters. Our diver-centered facilities include private lockers, a wet area, and an air-conditioned camera room to ensure your comfort and safety throughout the course.

Tailored Rescue Training

Your Rescue Diver Course

The course spans three days and includes pool training and open water dives designed to simulate emergency situations. Each session emphasizes diver safety, risk assessment, and rescue planning. From beginner rescue scenarios to complex underwater problem-solving, our instructors ensure that every participant gains confidence and competence. By the end of the course, you will have the skills to assist other divers in distress and manage emergencies efficiently.

Facilities Built for Divers

Dive Center Excellence

Our dive center is fully equipped to support rescue diver training. It features spacious wet areas, private lockers, an air-conditioned camera room, and a diver-friendly environment. Whether reviewing footage or preparing gear, our facilities make the training experience seamless and enjoyable.

FAQ

Rescue Diver FAQ

Do I need prior rescue training?

Yes, you should be a certified diver (Open Water or equivalent) before enrolling in the PADI Rescue Diver course.

What skills will I learn?

You’ll gain techniques to prevent and manage dive emergencies, perform rescues, and assist fellow divers safely.

How large are the training groups?

Groups are kept small, maximum two students per instructor, for personalized guidance and hands-on practice.

Where does the course take place?

Training includes controlled pool sessions and open water dives in the clear, pristine waters of West Sumbawa.

What facilities support the course?

Our dive center provides private lockers, a wet area, and an air-conditioned camera room to keep your gear and footage safe
