Drift Diving at Mocean Beach Resort

Experience the thrill of drift diving in the clear, current-swept waters around West Sumbawa. At Mocean Beach Resort, our PADI-certified guides lead small groups through underwater channels where gentle to moderate currents carry you effortlessly past vibrant reefs and schooling fish. These boat dives showcase soft corals, reef sharks, turtles, and pelagic species while you glide along with the flow. With a maximum of two divers per instructor, you’ll enjoy a safe, relaxed, and unforgettable deep sea adventure.

Your Drift Diving Experience

Effortless Exploration

A typical drift diving day begins with a short boat ride to select sites where tides create smooth, manageable currents. After a detailed safety briefing and current check, you’ll descend with your guide and let the ocean carry you along spectacular reef walls and coral gardens. Our team monitors conditions carefully, adjusting each dive to ensure a balanced mix of excitement and comfort. Whether you’re a seasoned deep sea diver or new to current diving, our instructors will help you perfect buoyancy and drift techniques.

Facilities Built for Divers

Dive Center Excellence

Mocean Beach Resort is fully equipped to support drift diving adventures. Our dive center features private gear lockers, spacious wet areas, a dedicated rinse station, and an air-conditioned camera room for post-dive reviews. From pre-dive planning to surface pickup, every detail is designed to keep you safe, comfortable, and ready for the next underwater ride.

FAQ

Drift Diving FAQ

Who can try drift diving?

Certified divers with some open water experience are best suited for drift diving adventures.

How strong are the currents?

Currents range from gentle to moderate, designed to carry divers safely along reefs while providing excitement.

What marine life can I see?

Expect turtles, reef sharks, schooling fish, soft corals, and occasional pelagic species in crystal-clear waters.

How large are the dive groups?

Groups are limited to two divers per instructor for safety, personalized attention, and comfort.

Are the facilities equipped for drift dives?

Yes. Our dive center includes private gear lockers, wet areas, a rinse station, and an air-conditioned camera room.
