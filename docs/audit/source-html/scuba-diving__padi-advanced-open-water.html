The Next Step in Your Dive Evolution

PADI Advanced Open Water Course

The PADI Advanced Open Water course at Mocean Beach Resort builds your confidence and expands your abilities through five adventure dives including Deep Diving and Underwater Navigation. You’ll also select three elective dives, such as Peak Performance Buoyancy, Drift Diving, or Night Diving, each designed to strengthen your control, awareness, and enjoyment underwater. Every dive is a new challenge, from learning to use lights in the dark to fine-tuning your buoyancy for effortless movement. Whether your goal is certification or self-improvement, this course helps you discover new depths of skill and connection with the ocean.

FAQ

Advanced Open Water FAQ

Do I need to be certified before taking this course?

Yes. You must hold a PADI Open Water Diver certification (or equivalent) before enrolling.

How long does the course take?

Usually two to three days, depending on your chosen adventure dives and availability.

What’s included in the course package?

Five adventure dives, eLearning materials, full equipment use, instructor fees, certification processing, and gear rental discounts.

How deep will I dive?

The Deep Dive takes you to a maximum depth of 30 meters (100 feet) under professional supervision.

Can I choose which adventure dives I do?

Yes. Beyond the mandatory Deep and Navigation dives, you can choose electives like Drift, Peak Performance Buoyancy, or Night Diving.

What facilities does the Dive Center offer?

A fully equipped dive base with gear stations, camera rinse areas, modern boats, and professional staff, all ensuring safety, comfort, and quality.

What can non-divers do at Mocean Beach Resort?

Non-divers can explore pristine beaches, snorkel vibrant reefs, visit Agal Waterfall, or enjoy scenic walks through local rice fields.

How can I contribute to marine conservation?

Join our Dive Against Debris program or other eco-initiatives aimed at protecting the marine environment through hands-on participation.

What skills will I develop?

You’ll master deep dive planning, underwater navigation, buoyancy control, and night dive techniques, building the confidence and ability for any dive environment.
