Open Water & Advanced Courses

Explore the underwater world with courses designed for all levels. The PADI Open Water course lets beginners earn their first certification, while PADI Advanced Open Water develops new skills and dives to deeper sites. All courses at Mocean Beach Resort follow PADI standards, ensuring your scuba diving certification course is globally recognized. Our programs also include youth and specialty courses like PADI Junior Open Water for younger adventurers.

Our dive center is designed to provide a seamless training experience. Recharge Amid Sumbawa’s Shores

Your dive adventure doesn’t end when you surface. Mocean Beach Resort offers quiet beaches, breathtaking sunsets, and easy access to Sumbawa’s hidden gems. Spend an afternoon exploring nearby villages, trek to cascading waterfalls, or simply unwind with a massage after a day of diving. Dive Into Discovery

Life Beyond the Reef

Beyond the crystal-clear waters, Sumbawa invites you to explore its wild side. Hike scenic coastal trails, visit traditional fishing villages, or take a day trip to secluded beaches where you can swim, snorkel, and soak in untouched beauty. For those seeking culture, local markets and ceremonies reveal the island’s rich heritage. Whether you’re chasing adrenaline or peaceful escapes, every moment here adds a new layer to your Indonesian journey.

FAQ

Open Water Course FAQ

Do I need prior diving experience?

No. We offer courses for beginners, including Discover Scuba Diving and Junior Open Water, as well as advanced and specialty programs.

Which PADI certifications can I earn here?

Courses range from Open Water to Advanced Open Water, Rescue Diver, Peak Performance Buoyancy, and specialty courses, all recognized worldwide.

How big are the training groups?

We keep groups small, maximum two students per instructor, so everyone receives personal guidance and attention.

Is equipment provided for courses?

Yes. Our dive center provides high-quality gear, private lockers, and air-conditioned camera rooms for your comfort and safety.

What can I do between dives?

Relax on quiet beaches, explore nearby villages, trek waterfalls, or enjoy spa treatments to recharge before your next underwater adventure.

How much is the PADI Open Water course in Sumbawa?

IDR 7,590,000, or IDR 7,920,000 with the PADI e-learning credit included.
