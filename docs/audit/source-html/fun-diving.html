Daily Fun Diving in West Sumbawa

Dive Into Daily Discovery

Explore Sumbawa's Reefs, One Day at a Time

Experience the thrill of Sumbawa’s underwater world with Mocean’s daily fun dives, perfect for travelers who want adventure without the comitment of a full dive package. Our expert local guides lead small groups of up to four certified divers, ensuring safety, comfort, and personal attention. Each trip reveals new coral gardens, schooling fish, and the chance to spot manta rays or eagle rays in clear 10 – 15 meter visibility. Between dives, relax on our custom-built boat’s sun deck and soak in the beauty of Indonesia’s untouched coastline.

Personalized Dive Adventures

Design Your Perfect Dive Trip

Every diver’s journey is unique. Mocean Beach Resort offers customizable dive adventures to match your schedule and experience level. Choose from single-day excursions, three-day mini packages, or week-long immersions, each including guided boat dives and expert instruction. Elective dives can focus on specialties such as Drift Diving, Night Diving, or Peak Performance Buoyancy, allowing you to shape your underwater experience exactly how you want it. Safety, comfort, and unforgettable marine encounters are guaranteed every step of the way.

Beyond the Reef

Relax, Recharge, and Discover Sumbawa

Your dive adventure doesn’t end when you surface. Mocean Beach Resort offers quiet beaches, breathtaking sunsets, and easy access to Sumbawa’s hidden gems. Spend an afternoon exploring nearby villages, trek to cascading waterfalls, or simply unwind with a massage after a day of diving. Whether you crave exploration or pure relaxation, our boutique setting lets you enjoy Indonesia’s natural beauty at your own pace. Every stay is designed to refresh your body and inspire your next dive.

Discover Island Magic

Experience More Than Diving

Beyond the crystal-clear waters, Sumbawa invites you to explore its wild side. Hike scenic coastal trails, visit traditional fishing villages, or take a day trip to secluded beaches where you can swim, snorkel, and soak in untouched beauty. For those seeking culture, local markets and ceremonies reveal the island’s rich heritage. Whether you’re chasing adrenaline or peaceful escapes, every moment here adds a new layer to your Indonesian journey.

FAQ

Fun Diving FAQ

How many divers are in each group?

We limit groups to a maximum of four certified divers per guide for a safer, more personal experience.

Can I book a short dive trip?

Yes. Packages start from a 3-day, 6-dive plan, with options for single-day outings or longer stays.

Are PADI courses available?

Absolutely. We offer everything from beginner Open Water to advanced Divemaster training.

What amenities does the dive center provide?

Expect private lockers, a spacious wet area, an air-conditioned camera room, and a custom-built dive boat with sun deck.

Do I need to bring my own gear?

No. Quality rental gear is available, though you’re welcome to bring your own equipment if preferred.
