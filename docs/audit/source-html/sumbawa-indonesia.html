Kopi Langit, Bintang Bano Dam and Ai Mual Waterfall

Kopi Langit • Bintang Bano Dam • Ai Mual Waterfall
Begin with a gentle hike to Kopi Langit, a coffee-plantation viewpoint overlooking jungle hills. Continue to Bintang Bano Dam for sweeping reservoir views before cooling off in Ai Mual’s crystal-clear waterfall pool. Lunch included. Minimum 2 people.

Full day · Lunch included · Minimum 2 people · Book this tour on WhatsApp

Agal Waterfall Trek

For those chasing adventure off the beaten path, the Agal Waterfall Trek is a full-day journey into the heart of Sumbawa’s wild interior. You’ll wind through jungle trails, hop across rivers, and feel the lush greenery surround you at every step. The trail leads to the dramatic, multi-tiered Agal Waterfall, a perfect spot to pause, take it all in, and snap a few photos.

Full day · Guided jungle trek with river crossings · Book this tour on WhatsApp

Read the Agal Waterfall Guide

Kalela Waterfall, Rice Field & Coconut Plantation

This half-day adventure blends nature, culture, and a little playful fun. Start with a dip in a natural pool complete with rope swings, then pause for photos at vibrant emerald rice fields. Continue to a nearby coconut grove where fresh coconuts await for a refreshing break. It’s a perfect mix of scenery, local flavor, and light adventure, ideal for a relaxed yet memorable Sumbawa escape. Minimum 2 people.

Half day · Minimum 2 people · Book this tour on WhatsApp

See the Rice Field Walk

Culture & Nature Encounters

Step into the rhythms of West Sumbawa life on this short yet enriching tour. Wander through sun-dappled coconut plantations, watch skilled artisans weave vibrant sarongs by hand, and discover the age-old practices of seaweed farming along the coast. It’s a gentle dive into local culture and nature, a memorable snapshot of island life with no minimum group size.

Short tour · No minimum group size · Book this tour on WhatsApp

Danau Lebo Lake Canoeing

Glide across a calm, palm-fringed lake with mountains stretching into the distance, where every turn feels like a postcard. Observe local fishermen casting their nets, experience the peaceful rhythm of daily life, and soak in the tranquil surroundings. This gentle, approximately four-hour adventure offers a perfect balance of nature, culture, and relaxation.

About 4 hours · Book this tour on WhatsApp

Read About Danau Lebo

Village Above the Clouds & Danau Lebo

Venture into the mountains for a truly breathtaking viewpoint above the clouds, where the island stretches out below in a patchwork of green. After soaking in the panorama, descend to Danau Lebo for a serene canoe ride across its tranquil waters, surrounded by nature’s quiet beauty. This experience blends awe-inspiring scenery with peaceful reflection, a highlight for any Indonesia tour itinerary. No minimum group size.

No minimum group size · Book this tour on WhatsApp

Read About Danau Lebo

Three Island Tour

Spend a full day island-hopping along Sumbawa’s pristine coastline, exploring three secluded islets that feel worlds away from the crowds. Snorkel among vibrant coral reefs, discover hidden beaches, and soak in the turquoise waters that define this untouched part of Indonesia. Perfect for travelers linking a Sumbawa–Bali route or adding an extra layer of adventure to a wider Indonesian itinerary. Minimum 2 people.

Full day · Snorkeling included · Minimum 2 people · Book this tour on WhatsApp

What to Expect

Guided experiences: Local guides share stories, hidden spots, and authentic touches on every Sumbawa tour.

Flexible options: From half-day cultural visits to full-day treks, choose tours that match your pace and interests.

Seasonal highlights: Some waterfalls are best after the rains—our team will advise on timing for the most scenic experience.

Pro Tips

Book tours in advance to secure guides and transport, especially in peak season. Wear sturdy shoes for hikes and bring a swimsuit for waterfall dips. Bring small cash donations for farmers or weavers when sampling coconuts or watching sarong making

FAQ

Sumbawa Tours FAQ

Do the tours include transport and lunch?

Yes, tours include a guide and transport from the resort. Full-day trips such as Agal Waterfall and Kopi Langit include lunch.

Is there a minimum group size?

Some tours need at least two people, including Kopi Langit, Kalela and the Three Island Tour. Culture and Nature Encounters and the Village Above the Clouds run for any group size.

How do I book a tour?

Message us on WhatsApp at +62 821 4419 1647 with the tours you’d like and your dates. We’ll fit them around your diving or surfing.

Book a Tour from Mocean

Every tour leaves from Mocean Beach Resort with a local guide and driver. Most guests book one or two during their stay and save the rest of the days for the water. Message us with the tours you like and your dates, and we’ll put them on days when the surf or diving is quieter.
