Snorkeling in Sumbawa at Mocean Beach Resort

Serene Currents, Vibrant Underwater Life

Snorkel the House Reef or Head Out by Boat

Slip into the clear, turquoise waters of Sumbawa and discover vibrant reefs just steps from your bungalow. Snorkeling at Mocean Beach Resort offers effortless access to coral gardens alive with reef fish, rays, and turtles. Whether you explore our calm house reef or join a guided boat trip to nearby sites, every swim reveals new marine wonders. With warm tropical waters, snorkeling in Sumbawa is ideal for families, first-timers, and seasoned ocean lovers alike.

Seasons & Conditions

Snorkel Gear & Facilities

Our team provides everything you need for a relaxed day of snorkeling in Sumbawa—quality masks, fins, and snorkels, plus flotation vests for extra comfort. After your swim, rinse your gear in the dedicated wet area or review photos in the air-conditioned camera room. Whether you bring your own equipment or rent ours, the Mocean staff ensures your gear is ready for the next adventure.

Our Snorkel Sites

Mocean House Reef

Walk in from the beach at Pantai Moro. Shallow coral with reef fish, and turtles on a good day. Rent a mask, fins and snorkel for IDR 200,000 a day and go whenever the tide suits.

Outer Reefs by Boat

Our half-day boat trip heads to reefs off the West Sumbawa coast with more coral cover and bigger fish. A guide swims with the group and points out rays, clownfish and parrotfish.

Island Snorkeling on the Three Island Tour

For a full day, join the Three Island Tour and snorkel around small islets off the coast, with beach stops in between.

Post-Dive Bliss

Relax, Recharge, and Discover Sumbawa

Your snorkeling adventure doesn’t end when you leave the water. Mocean Beach Resort offers quiet beaches, breathtaking sunsets, and easy access to Sumbawa’s hidden gems. Spend an afternoon exploring nearby villages, trek to cascading waterfalls, or simply unwind with a massage after a day in the sea. Whether you crave exploration or pure relaxation, our boutique setting lets you enjoy Indonesia’s natural beauty at your own pace. Every stay is designed to refresh your body and inspire your next adventure.

Wander Beyond Waves

Experience More Than Snorkeling

Beyond the crystal-clear waters, Sumbawa invites you to explore its wild side. Hike scenic coastal trails, visit traditional fishing villages, or take a day trip to secluded beaches where you can swim, snorkel, and soak in untouched beauty. For those seeking culture, local markets and ceremonies reveal the island’s rich heritage. Whether you’re chasing adrenaline or peaceful escapes, every moment here adds a new layer to your Indonesian journey.

FAQ

Snorkeling FAQ

Do I need previous snorkeling experience?

Not at all. Our calm house reef and guided trips are perfect for beginners and families.

When is the best time to snorkel?

April to October offers the clearest water and calmest seas, though the house reef is enjoyable year-round

Is gear provided at the resort?

Yes. We rent quality masks, fins, snorkels, and flotation vests, or you can bring your own equipment.

Are guided boat trips available?

Absolutely. Join our half-day outer reef excursions to explore remote coral gardens and deeper marine life.

What marine life might I see?

Expect turtles, rays, clownfish, parrotfish, and vibrant corals in pristine, clear waters.

How much does snorkeling cost at Mocean?

The guided boat snorkel trip is IDR 676,500 per person. Gear rental for the house reef is IDR 200,000 a day.

Is snorkeling in Sumbawa good for kids?

Yes, on calm days. The house reef has shallow areas close to shore, and we have flotation vests in small sizes. An adult should stay with children in the water.
