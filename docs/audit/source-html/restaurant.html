Enjoy a relaxed breakfast, a productive business lunch, or a memorable dinner in a warm and inviting setting
Mutiara by Mocean

Naturally Sourced, Lovingly Served

Mutiara by Mocean crafts every meal with local ingredients and culinary traditions from around the world, bringing fresh flavors to each dish. Whether you’re enjoying a peaceful breakfast, a leisurely lunch, or an intimate dinner, the carefully designed dining space ensures a balance of comfort and flavor, making every experience satisfying and memorable.

Open daily, 7am to 10pm. Message us on WhatsApp at +62 821 4419 1647 to book a table, or to set up a private dinner on the sand.

FAQ

Restaurant FAQ

What are Mutiara by Mocean's opening hours?

Mutiara is open every day from 7am to 10pm.

Are there vegetarian and vegan options?

Yes. The menu has vegetarian dishes such as tempe goreng, falafel wraps and bakwan, and the kitchen can adapt others for vegans.

Are menu prices inclusive of tax?

Menu prices are in thousand rupiah and exclude 11% local tax and 10% service.

How do I book a table?

Message us on WhatsApp at +62 821 4419 1647. Booking ahead is a good idea for dinner and for private beachfront tables.
