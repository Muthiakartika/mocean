Night Diving at Mocean

Experience the magic of night diving at Mocean Beach Resort, where the reef transforms into a luminous world after dusk. As an enthusiastic night diver, you’ll venture out under the stars for guided dives with a torch, exploring vibrant coral, nocturnal fishes, crustaceans, and creatures that only emerge in darkness. With just four divers per guide, night time scuba diving sessions are intimate, safe, and unforgettable. Our dive center provides the gear you need for night dives and prioritizes your safety with well-lit briefing zones, secure rinse points, and specialized lighting equipment.

Preparing for the Night Dive

Night Dive Safety & Essentials

Before the reef lights up under your dive torch, every night dive begins with a thorough safety briefing. Instructors review dive plans, currents, and tide conditions while checking your lighting and equipment. With these preparations, you’ll confidently descend into Sumbawa’s reefs after sunset. Typical dives last 45–65 minutes, giving you plenty of time to navigate the reef safely while discovering nocturnal marine life like sleeping parrotfish, crabs, and octopus.

Exploring the Reef After Dark

Your Night Dive Adventure

As the sun disappears, the underwater world transforms. Guided by Mocean’s experienced instructors, you’ll glide along coral walls illuminated by dive lights, revealing hidden reef activity and glowing bioluminescence when in season. Night diving brings a unique rhythm and perspective to Sumbawa’s waters, letting divers see behaviors and creatures not visible during the day. Each dive lasts around 45–65 minutes, offering both excitement and a calm, immersive experience under the stars.

FAQ

Night Diving FAQ

Who can join night dives?

Certified divers with some open water experience are ideal for night diving adventures.

What time do night dives start?

Night dives begin at dusk, shortly after sunset, with departure times adjusted to local conditions.

What marine life will I see?

Expect nocturnal reef creatures like shrimp, crabs, octopus, sleeping parrotfish, and seasonal bioluminescence.

How large are the dive groups?

Groups are limited to two divers per instructor for safety, comfort, and personalized guidance.

Is special equipment needed?

Yes. Professional torches, lighting gear, and pre-dive safety checks are provided for every night dive.
