Sumbawa's Tropical Beach Resort

Garden-View Rooms Steps from the Sand

Nestled on Sumbawa’s pristine coastline, Mocean Beach Resort offers tranquil garden-view accommodations just steps from the sea. Surrounded by tropical greenery, each room is designed for comfort and relaxation, blending natural elements with modern simplicity. Guests can enjoy attentive personal service, refreshing welcome drinks, and local insights to help make the most of their beachside escape. While the resort overlooks the ocean, the rooms open to lush gardens that capture the island’s calm, natural beauty.

Drifting into Days Chasing Sunsets

Days at this beach resort gently flow into peaceful evenings, inviting guests to gather and watch the sun dip below the horizon. As it sets, the sky blooms with colors, beginning a calming ritual. It’s a moment that highlights nature’s beauty and draws us closer to the serene surroundings.

This is a time for reflection or for sharing quiet moments with others. Each sunset carries a promise of peace, closing the day in the most beautiful way.

Right Beside the Sea

The essence of a stay at Mocean Beach Resort is being so close to the ocean. While our rooms look onto lush tropical gardens, the sound of waves is never far away, drifting into your space and setting the rhythm for your holiday.

Step outside and the beach is right there. The restaurant, pool, lounge, and yoga chalet all sit along the shoreline, making it easy to enjoy the sea breeze, ocean views, and unforgettable sunsets.

From quiet mornings to active afternoons, the ocean is always within reach. Whether you’re practicing yoga by the waves, sharing a meal with a sea view, or unwinding poolside, every moment connects you with the essence of seaside living.

Slow Days by the Sea

A Day at the Beach Resort

Mornings start with coffee at Mutiara looking out over Moro Beach. Some guests head out on the dive boat or paddle out at Northern Rights; others don’t leave the pool until lunch. Afternoons are for a walk along the 2-kilometre beach, a massage or a nap on the lanai. Then everyone ends up back at the shoreline for sunset and dinner.

FAQ

Beach Resort FAQ

Is Mocean Beach Resort right on the beach?

Yes. The pool, restaurant, lounge and yoga chalet sit on the shoreline of Pantai Moro. The rooms face tropical gardens a few steps back from the sand.

Do the rooms have sea views?

The rooms look onto the gardens. For sea views, the pool deck and Mutiara restaurant face the ocean directly.

Is the resort good for families?

Yes. Rooms take up to three guests and a cot or child’s fold-out bed can be added. The house reef has shallow snorkeling and the pool is right by the beach.
