Rooms and Bungalows at Mocean Beach Resort

Experience comfort tailored to every traveler, with room options and amenities to suit all preferences
Accommodation

Deluxe Garden View Bungalows

Each of our six bungalows offers 39 sqm of indoor space and an 11 sqm private lanai, built using regional materials by skilled local artisans. Inside, you’ll find custom-made Jepara teak furniture, premium King Koil beds, and a private bathroom with a rain shower and sustainable amenities. For year-round comfort, each bungalow is equipped with air conditioning and a ceiling fan, while the lanai provides a peaceful spot to relax.

Which Room Is Right for You?

Choose a Deluxe Garden View Bungalow if you want space and a private lanai to sit out on after a dive: 39 m² inside plus an 11 m² lanai, Jepara teak furniture and a King Koil pillowtop. Choose a Standard Room for the same bed size and rain shower at a lower rate, without the lanai. Both sleep up to three and both have air conditioning.

FAQ

Rooms FAQ

How many rooms does Mocean Beach Resort have?

Ten: six Deluxe Garden View Bungalows with private lanai and four Standard Rooms.

Can I bring a child?

Yes. Rooms take up to three guests, and one cot or child’s fold-out bed can be added. Rollaway beds aren’t available.

Is the tap water drinkable?

Every tap runs reverse-osmosis purified water, so you can refill your bottle in the room.
