Getting to Mocean Beach Resort

Plan your arrival with confidence, with flexible transfer options designed to suit every traveler’s schedule and style
Your adventure begins the moment you depart

Journey to Mocean Beach Resort

Mocean Beach Resort is on Pantai Moro in West Sumbawa, one island east of Lombok. Most guests arrive one of two ways: a flight into Sumbawa Besar, or a ferry from Lombok to Poto Tano. We run private pickups from both. For every route from Bali, including the overland ferries and helicopter, see our full guide to getting to Sumbawa.

Airport Pickup from Sumbawa Besar (SWQ)

Begin your trip at Sultan Muhammad Kaharuddin III Airport (SWQ) in Sumbawa Besar, the main hub for reaching this side of the island. From here, Mocean offers a seamless private transfer service around 2 hours and 15 minutes of scenic driving through the stunning landscapes of West Sumbawa. The route is often highlighted in travel to Sumbawa Utama guides, known for its dramatic hillsides, winding coastal views, and untouched countryside. Along the way you’ll pass through traditional villages and lush greenery, giving you a glimpse of local life before arriving at the serene setting of Mocean Resort.

Enjoy scenic island views as you arrive by boat

Ferry Pickup from Poto Tano

If you prefer to travel by sea, you can arrive through Poto Tano, the ferry port linking Lombok with Sumbawa. The crossing offers scenic views of the surrounding islands, making the journey part of the adventure. From Poto Tano, it’s just a 30-minute car ride to Mocean Resort, ideal for guests arriving from Lombok or those enjoying an island-hopping itinerary. With ocean breezes and our team ready to welcome you, this route combines convenience with a memorable start to your stay.

Pro Tips for a Smooth Arrival

Plan your arrival in advance to make your journey as smooth as possible. Check flight options into Sumbawa Besar Airport (SWQ) or review ferry schedules from Lombok to ensure you avoid unnecessary delays. For longer rides, it’s a good idea to pack a few essentials such as light snacks, bottled water, and sunscreen so you stay comfortable along the way. Don’t forget to share your exact arrival details with our team so transfers are arranged and waiting for you upon landing or docking. Whenever possible, aim to arrive during daylight hours, as it not only makes navigation easier but also allows you to take in the stunning island scenery right from the start. By preparing ahead, your first impression of Mocean Beach Resort will be relaxed, seamless, and truly memorable.
