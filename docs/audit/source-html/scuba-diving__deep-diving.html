Deep Diving

Dive Into Greater Depths

PADI Deep Diving

At Mocean Beach Resort, our deep diving courses immerse certified divers into the thrill of exploring the ocean’s deeper realms. These experiences are designed for adventurous divers ready to become skilled deep divers under the guidance of experienced instructors. Dive with just two divers per guide for a personalized learning adventure. Expect encounters with vibrant marine life, including manta rays, reef sharks, and eagle rays, at depths reaching up to 40 meters. Our diver-centered facilities include private lockers, a wet area, and an air-conditioned camera room to ensure comfort and safety throughout every deep sea diving session.

Tailored Deep Diving Experiences

Your Deep Diving Course

Each course spans two days and includes both theory and practical dives designed to develop deep diver skills. Divers practice descending, buoyancy control, navigation, and emergency procedures at increased depths. Instruction emphasizes safety, risk management, and situational awareness to ensure every diver feels confident. By the end of the course, participants will earn the PADI Deep Diver certification and have the skills to safely explore deeper marine environments.

After the Dive

Relax, Recharge, and Discover Sumbawa

Your dive adventure doesn’t end when you surface. Mocean Beach Resort offers quiet beaches, breathtaking sunsets, and easy access to Sumbawa’s hidden gems. Spend an afternoon exploring nearby villages, trek to cascading waterfalls, or simply unwind with a spa treatment after a day of diving. Whether you crave exploration or pure relaxation, our boutique setting lets you enjoy Indonesia’s natural beauty at your own pace. Every stay is designed to refresh your body and inspire your next dive.

FAQ

Deep Diving FAQ

Who can join the Deep Diving course?

Certified divers with Open Water or Advanced Open Water qualifications can participate.

What depths will I explore?

Courses safely take divers down to 40 meters, with gradual skill-building and supervision.

How large are the training groups?

Groups are capped at two divers per instructor for personalized guidance and attention.

What skills are covered in the course?

Divers practice deep descent techniques, buoyancy control, navigation, and emergency procedures.

Are the facilities equipped for deep diving?

Yes. Our dive center has private lockers, wet areas, and an air-conditioned camera room to support your training.
